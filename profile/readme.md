## What this is
Human Automatica is a personal AI systems practice. The work spans areas where structure matters.

* **Agent ecosystems**: Multi-model workflows with defined execution lanes, task specs, and completion records.
* **Knowledge architecture**: Systems to ensure that insights from one project are not lost in the next.
* **Governance frameworks**: Promotion rules, scope contracts, and decision records that keep systems coherent over time.
* **Tooling and observability**: Dashboards and scaffolding that make the work visible and manageable.

---

## Learned principles
These are the patterns that keep the automation shaped and governed.

* **Collaborative by design**: Good automation starts with conversation. The structure gets worked out together and then the system executes.
* **Architecture before execution**: No agent runs well without knowing exactly what it is supposed to do.
* **Governance by design**: Rules and scope boundaries are documented rather than assumed. Undocumented systems drift.
* **Verification before trust**: The focus is on deterministic and verifiable behavior.
